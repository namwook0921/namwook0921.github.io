About this site

CS 180 is Berkeley's introduction to computer vision and computational photography: how images are formed, how to manipulate them, and how to get a computer to make sense of them. The course is built around hands-on projects, and this site is where mine live.

Everything here is written and photographed by me unless noted otherwise. Photos are shown at reduced resolution for page weight — click any image to open it larger.

About This Site

CS 180 is Berkeley's introduction to computer vision and computational photography: how images are formed, how to manipulate them, and how to get a computer to make sense of them. The course runs on hands-on projects, and this site is where mine live — one page per project, with the photos, the results, and the reasoning behind them.

Everything here is written and photographed by me unless noted otherwise. Photos are shown at reduced resolution to keep pages light; click any of them to open the full-size version.
